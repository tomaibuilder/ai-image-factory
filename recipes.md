# Recipes

Named styles. Fill the `[brackets]` from the brief and the `{braces}` from `brand/BRAND.md`. Add your own below the starters. A recipe can point at a reference image to riff on.

## 1. Editorial cover

**Look:** calm and premium. Plain `{background}` paper, a large headline in `{headline font}` and `{ink}`, one simple line illustration of an object from the topic in `{accent}`, lots of empty space.
**Use for:** guides, lead magnets, articles, anything that should feel authoritative.
**Prompt skeleton:**
```
Editorial magazine-style cover, [size ratio]. Plain {background} paper with a faint grain. Large headline "[HEADLINE]" in {headline font}, colour {ink}, set top-left. Below it, a single clean line illustration of [object from the topic] drawn in {accent}, thin even strokes. Generous empty space, nothing else on the page. Calm, premium, print quality.
```

## 2. Photo and headline

**Look:** a realistic photo of the reader's own world, dark and moody with one warm light source, a bold headline in `{accent}` over the darkest part of the frame.
**Use for:** stories, results, promotions, "this is what the work looks like".
**Prompt skeleton:**
```
Cinematic photograph, [size ratio]. [Scene from the reader's world, e.g. a dentist's front desk after closing], lit by one warm practical light, deep shadows, shallow depth of field. Bold headline "[HEADLINE]" in a heavy condensed sans-serif, colour {accent}, set over the darkest area so it reads clearly. Photoreal, no other text.
```

## 3. Big number

**Look:** one number, huge, in `{accent}`, a short label under it, and a small illustration that shows what is being counted.
**Use for:** a real result or stat. Never use a number that is not in the brief.
**Prompt skeleton:**
```
Bold stat graphic, [size ratio], {background} background. The number "[REAL NUMBER]" huge, in a heavy sans-serif, colour {accent}. Under it, the short label "[WHAT IT COUNTS]" in {ink}. Beside the number, a small flat illustration of [the thing counted, e.g. a stack of booked calendar slots]. Clean, high contrast, no other text.
```

## 4. Before and after split

**Look:** the frame split down the middle. Left: the messy "before" scene, desaturated. Right: the same place, clean and working, in full colour with `{accent}` highlights. One short label on each side.
**Use for:** transformations, tool or process comparisons, "what changed".
**Prompt skeleton:**
```
Split-screen image, [size ratio], divided vertically down the centre. Left half: [the before scene, e.g. a cluttered desk buried in paper invoices], desaturated and grey, small label "[BEFORE LABEL]". Right half: the same scene [after, e.g. a clear desk with one laptop showing a tidy dashboard], full colour with {accent} highlights, small label "[AFTER LABEL]". Same camera angle on both sides. Photoreal.
```
